# Volunteer Consent Form — KYA-Trust study

**Project:** KYA-Trust, Team Versae (Kartik Acharya, Prachi Kumawat), IIIT Kota
**Purpose:** Entry for the Perfios x Techfest IIT Bombay hackathon. We are building a system that tells apart people, scripted bots and AI agents using a fake banking website.

## What you will do
You will use a **mock banking website** running on our laptop for about 10–15 minutes per session: log in with a test account we give you, check a balance, add a payee, and send fake money. **No real money or real bank account is involved.** Some sessions are role-plays where a chatbot pretends to be the bank and asks you to move money. You can stop any role-play at any time.

## What we record
- Timing of mouse movement, clicks, scrolling and key presses
- Which page and button you used (by an internal ID)
- The type of key pressed (letter, number, backspace), **never which key**
- Coarse device info (e.g. "laptop", "Chrome")

## What we never record
- What you type, including passwords or names
- Your screen, camera or microphone
- Your real name in the data: you appear only as an ID like P07

## How the data is used and shared
- To train and test our models during the hackathon.
- **The sanitized data (timings and IDs only, as listed above) will be published with our submission** so judges can reproduce our results.
- This form, which has your name, is kept privately by the team and never published.

## Your rights
Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time, and you can ask us to delete your sessions **before 6 December 2026**, after which the dataset is frozen for evaluation.
